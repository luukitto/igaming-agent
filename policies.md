# Platform policies (fictional, for the demo)

## KYC: identity verification before withdrawals
Players may deposit and play before identity verification, but no withdrawal is paid out until KYC status is "verified". Withdrawals requested while KYC is "pending" or "rejected" are declined with reason kyc_not_verified. Action: ask the player to upload a government ID and proof of address; support may resubmit the withdrawal once verification is complete. Do not manually approve withdrawals for unverified players.

## Bonus wagering requirement
Bonus funds carry a 35x wagering requirement: the player must place bets totalling 35 times the bonus amount before withdrawing. Example: a 100 bonus requires 3500 in total stakes. Withdrawals requested before the requirement is met are declined with reason wagering_requirement_not_met. Action: tell the player how much they have wagered and how much remains, or offer to forfeit the bonus (and its winnings) so the cash balance can be withdrawn.

## Responsible gambling: risk indicators
Escalate an account to the responsible gambling team when it shows several of these indicators: deposits rising sharply over a short period (for example doubling week over week), frequent play between midnight and 5 am, repeated deposits soon after losses (chasing losses), or hitting deposit limits. Action: escalate for a safer-gambling interaction; do not offer bonuses or promotions to this player; consider a cooling-off period.

## Deposit limits
Players can set daily, weekly and monthly deposit limits. A deposit that would exceed a limit is declined with reason deposit_limit_reached. Limit increases take effect only after a 24-hour cooling period; decreases apply immediately. Repeatedly hitting a limit is a responsible gambling indicator.

## Self-exclusion
A self-excluded player cannot deposit, bet or log in for the chosen exclusion period (6 months minimum). Deposit attempts are declined with reason self_exclusion_active. Action: never reopen the account early or send marketing to the player; log the attempt and notify the responsible gambling team. Support must not help the player bypass the exclusion.

## Withdrawals: processing times
Approved withdrawals are processed within 24 hours to e-wallets and within 3 to 5 business days to bank cards. Pending withdrawals can be cancelled by the player until they are processed.
