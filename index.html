            Words you might see

            KYC (Know Your Customer)
            The identity check. Before a player can withdraw money, they must upload a government ID and a proof of address. If the check isn't finished, withdrawals are declined.
            Wagering requirement
            A bonus comes with a rule: the player must bet a set amount (for example 35 times the bonus) before they can withdraw. Until they have, withdrawals are declined.
            Responsible Gambling (RG)
            Our duty to protect players from gambling harm. The RG team are specialists who review worrying accounts and talk to players about their gambling.
            Hand over to the RG team ("Escalate to RG")
            The player shows signs of gambling harm, so the RG team needs to look at the account. Don't try to handle it alone: make sure the "Send to Responsible Gambling team" action gets approved, and stay calm and non-judgmental with the player.
            Risk score
            A number from 0 to 100 that adds up warning signs, such as: depositing much more than usual, playing mostly between midnight and 5 am, betting more after losing (chasing losses), cancelling a withdrawal and gambling the money, very long sessions, and hitting a deposit limit. Higher means more worrying.
            Self-exclusion
            The player asked us to stop them from gambling for a period. They can't deposit or play until it ends, even if they ask, and we must not encourage them to come back.
            Deposit limit
            The most a player can deposit per week. We can lower it straight away; raising it needs a cooling-off period, so it can't be done here.
            Block account
            The player can't log in or play. Used for fraud or abuse, not for gambling harm (that goes to the RG team).
